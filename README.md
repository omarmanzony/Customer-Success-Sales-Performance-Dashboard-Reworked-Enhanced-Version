<h1 align="center">Customer Success & Sales Performance Dashboard – Reworked & Enhanced Version</h1>

<p align="center">
An interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer success, customer feedback, sales performance, and Account Manager performance.
</p>

<h2>Project Overview</h2>

Customer Success & Sales Performance Dashboard – Reworked & Enhanced Version is an improved version of the original Customer Success & Sales Performance Dashboard.

The project focuses on analyzing customer feedback, customer classifications, Account Manager performance, response rates, and customer success indicators through an interactive Power BI solution.

The dashboard provides a structured analytical view that helps identify customer feedback patterns, compare Account Manager performance, and evaluate customer engagement and satisfaction.

<h2>Business Objectives</h2>

- Analyze overall customer performance and engagement.
- Measure customer response and positive feedback rates.
- Evaluate performance across Account Managers.
- Analyze customer feedback and identify major feedback categories.
- Compare positive performance across Account Managers.
- Analyze customers by industry and classification.
- Identify top and bottom performing industries.
- Analyze customer titles and customer distribution.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for customer success analysis.

<h2>Key Performance Indicators</h2>

The dashboard provides a set of core customer success and performance metrics including:

- Total Customers
- Positive Customers
- Positive Rate
- No Answer
- Didn't Call Them
- Response Rate

These KPIs provide a high-level view of customer engagement, response performance, and positive customer feedback.

<h2>Analytical Perspectives</h2>

<h3>Customer Success Performance</h3>

Provides an overview of customer feedback and Account Manager performance.

The analysis includes:

- Total Customers
- Positive Customers
- Positive Rate
- Response Rate
- Customer Feedback
- Customer distribution by Account Manager
- Positive performance by Account Manager
- Response performance by Account Manager

<h3>Customer Classification & Industry Analysis</h3>

Analyzes the customer base across different classifications and industries.

The analysis includes:

- Customer Classifications
- Top Industries
- Bottom Industries
- Customer Titles
- Customer distribution by classification
- Industry-level customer analysis

<h3>Customer Breakdown Analysis</h3>

Provides a detailed breakdown of customer performance using Account Manager and customer classification perspectives.

The analysis includes:

- Customer distribution by Account Manager
- Customer classification analysis
- Customer feedback categories
- Positive customer performance
- Account Manager performance comparison
- Customer-level analysis

<h3>Account Manager Performance</h3>

Evaluates customer success performance across Account Managers.

The analysis compares:

- Total Customers
- Positive Customers
- Positive Rate
- Response Rate
- Customer Feedback

The dashboard allows performance comparisons across individual Account Managers to identify differences in customer engagement and positive feedback.

<h2>Key Insights</h2>

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer success performance across Account Managers, customer classifications, industries, and feedback categories.

The analysis highlights differences in customer engagement, positive customer performance, and response rates across Account Managers.

The dashboard also provides visibility into customer feedback categories such as Positive, No Answer, Duplicate, No Feedback, Forgot The AM, Complaint, High Prices, and Left.

Customer classification and industry analysis provide additional perspectives for understanding the composition and characteristics of the customer base.

Detailed figures and visual breakdowns are available in the dashboard PDF.

<h2>Data Preparation & Transformation</h2>

The customer data was prepared and structured to create a dataset suitable for analysis and visualization in Microsoft Power BI.

The data preparation process supports customer performance analysis, feedback categorization, Account Manager comparisons, and KPI calculations used throughout the dashboard.
